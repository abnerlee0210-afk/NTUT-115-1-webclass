Blue Hour

Draw an analog clock in HTML Canvas.

Canvas exercise

  1. Draw the face. Use arc() and a radial gradient for the dial. Place Roman numerals at the four quarter hours.
  2. Draw 60 tick marks. Give every fifth mark extra length and weight. Keep the 48 marks between hours short and thin.
  3. Shape three hands. Draw a wide, faceted polygon for the hour hand and a narrower, longer polygon for the minute hand. Give the thin second hand a round counterweight.
  4. Make it run. Check your angles at 10:08:37, then draw the local time once a second. Show the same time as HH:MM:SS on the dial.
