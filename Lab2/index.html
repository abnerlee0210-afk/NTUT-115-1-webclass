01 / EXPLORE

Where history lives in every corner.

ANCIENT CAPITAL HERITAGE

Kyoto serves as the cultural heart of Japan, preserving thousands of classical Buddhist temples, Shinto shrines, and traditional wooden houses. Walking through its streets feels like traveling back in time.

From seasonal cherry blossoms in spring to vibrant maple leaves in autumn, every season paints Kyoto with a fresh artistic palette.

"In Kyoto, every narrow alley holds centuries of quiet beauty."

02 / DISTRICTS

Wander through ancient pathways.

HISTORIC CHARM OF GION & HIGASHIYAMA

Explore the cobblestone streets of Higashiyama and the lantern-lit alleys of Gion. Here, traditional craft shops and tea rooms line the roads leading to sacred hillsides.

Take a relaxed stroll during early evening to catch a glimpse of Geiko and Maiko hurrying to their traditional appointments along wooden Machiya townhouses.

The true essence of Kyoto is found when you slow down and savor every quiet moment.
